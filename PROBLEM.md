# CITEMAS Portal — Problem Statement

## Overview
CITEMAS (College of Information Technology Education Multimedia Arts Society) is a student-led organization at PHINMA UI dedicated to fostering creativity, digital arts, animation, videography, photography, and multimedia technologies.

## Problem Context
Students, faculty advisers, officers, and community members need a centralized, cohesive digital platform to:
1. Showcase student portfolios and creative works across multiple media disciplines.
2. Manage club membership applications, approvals, and role-based permissions seamlessly.
3. Organize, publish, and track studio events, workshops, and creative exhibits.
4. Facilitate community engagement and collaboration across creative specializations.

## Target Audience
- **Students / Applicants / Members**: Looking to join guilds, showcase multimedia projects, attend workshops, and collaborate.
- **Officers / Leadership**: Reviewing member admissions, curating event listings, and moderating community submissions.
- **Faculty Advisers / Administrators**: Overseeing institutional compliance, approving staff roles, and tracking student organization activities.
