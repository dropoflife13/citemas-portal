# CITEMAS Portal — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Summary
The CITEMAS Portal is the official web platform for the CITEMAS creative guild at PHINMA UI. It serves as the community hub for membership onboarding, creative portfolios, events, and organizational governance.

## 2. Core Personas & Roles
- **Guest / Unauthenticated User**: Public visitor viewing public events, gallery highlights, and club information.
- **Student / User**: Registered student eligible to apply for membership across creative specializations.
- **Applicant**: Student with a submitted membership application currently under review.
- **Member**: Approved creative guild member with access to the member directory, portfolio submissions, and exclusive events.
- **Officer**: Student leader with capabilities to review applications, manage events, and update member records.
- **Adviser / Teacher / Super Admin**: Executive staff supervising organization operations, administrative tools, and compliance.

## 3. Core Modules & Capabilities
1. **Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Secure registration, login, role progression (`user` → `applicant` → `member` / `officer` / `admin`).
2. **Member Applications**: Multi-stage application submission, specialization selection, portfolio links, motivation statement, and review workflow.
3. **Events & Workshops Management**: Public and member-only events, RSVP tracking, scheduling, and archiving.
4. **Student Portfolios**: Creative work showcase across specializations (Traditional Arts, Digital Arts, Animation, Videography, Photography, Voice Acting).
5. **Community Directory**: Searchable, filterable registry of active members, officers, and alumni.
6. **Administration & Audit**: Activity logging, application approvals, user management, and platform analytics.
